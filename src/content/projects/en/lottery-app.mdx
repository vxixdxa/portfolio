## The Challenge

iNUM lottery platform was facing user attrition — players reported the existing interface was cumbersome and the betting process too lengthy, while competitors had already launched more modernized experiences. This was a design challenge requiring balance between "feature complexity" and "operational simplicity."

### Core Pain Points

- The betting flow required 6 steps to complete a single bet
- Chaotic information hierarchy across multiple lottery types made it hard for players to find their preferred games
- Extremely high information density on pages created steep learning curves for new players

---

## Design Strategy

### 1. User Behavior Analysis

I first analyzed existing users' heatmaps and usage paths, discovering that 70% of users only played 2-3 fixed lottery types. This insight directly influenced my information architecture — splitting "Favorites" and "Explore" into two independent entry points.

### 2. Betting Flow Simplification

Redesigned the betting process from 6 steps down to 3: **Select Numbers → Confirm Amount → Submit**. Introduced a "Quick Pick" feature enabling repeat bets with a single tap.

### 3. Visual System Establishment

Built a comprehensive Design Library unifying the visual language across 10+ lottery types, enabling players to quickly identify game categories through colors and icons alone.

---

## Key Design Decision

For the number selection interface, I faced a core trade-off: **"Numeric Keypad Style"** vs. **"Ball Selection Style."**

I ultimately chose the numeric keypad approach, because user testing revealed that players' mental models were closer to "calculator input" rather than "lottery machine ball picking." The keypad layout improved operation speed by **40%**.

---

## Impact

- Bet completion rate significantly improved (steps reduced from 6 to 3)
- First-time player successful bet rate improved dramatically
- Design Library reduced design time for subsequent new lottery type launches by 60%
