## Overview

Reimagined the website experience for a medical aesthetics brand — using clearer information architecture, a warm yet professional visual language, and well-defined booking paths to help the brand build trust and ease users' uncertainty before reaching out for a consultation.

---

## Scope of Work

Website Structure · User Flow · Visual Direction · UI Design · Responsive Design · CTA Planning · Design Handoff

---

## The Challenge

HEAL Aesthetic is a premium medical aesthetics center with professional treatments and services, but its original website couldn't fully convey the brand's positioning or the value of its services. While browsing treatment information, potential clients easily hesitated — held back by clinical jargon, insufficient information, or the lack of clear calls to action.

The core challenge of this project was this: how to translate professional medical aesthetics services into a website experience that ordinary users can easily understand, while striking a balance between brand quality, information transparency, and the booking action.

### Core Pain Points

- The original website's visual style was inconsistent with the brand's positioning and failed to convey a premium, professional, and reassuring feel.
- The booking and consultation paths weren't clear enough, leaving users unsure of what to do next after browsing.
- The site lacked enough trust elements, so it couldn't effectively lower the psychological barrier of approaching medical aesthetics for the first time.

---

## Design Process

I was responsible for the site's information architecture, user browsing flows, brand visual direction, UI design, and responsive page design. Throughout the process, I focused on turning the professional content of medical aesthetics services into a clear, friendly, and trustworthy digital experience.

---

## Design Strategy

### Establishing a Warm Yet Professional Brand Feel

Visually, I steered away from an overly cold clinical feel, choosing a soft, warm, and clean palette of beige and earth tones for the brand's primary visual identity. The overall interface aims to convey reassurance, refinement, and a sense of aesthetics — not just standardized medical information.

This visual direction lets users sense the brand's professionalism and approachability the moment they enter the site, reducing the sense of distance toward medical aesthetics services.

### Restructuring the Treatment Information Architecture

I broke the originally dense treatment content into more readable information modules — including treatment introductions, who each is suitable for, expected results, process explanations, and FAQs. Users can understand the services step by step according to their own needs, without having to digest a large volume of professional information all at once.

This makes the site not just a brand showcase, but a tool that helps users make consultation decisions.

### Optimizing the Booking & Consultation Path

I placed clear calls to action throughout the pages so that, after understanding the treatment content, users can naturally move into the booking or consultation flow. The CTAs aren't designed to force conversion; instead, they sit at the key moments where a user is likely to feel a need, helping them smoothly take the next step.

---

## Impact

- Re-established the brand's sense of professionalism and trust online.
- Made treatment information easier to understand, reducing the burden of reading professional content.
- Built clearer booking and consultation paths, letting users naturally complete the next step.
- Through a consistent visual language, brought the website more in line with the brand's positioning and service value.
