## The Challenge

HEAL Aesthetic is a premium medical aesthetics center with quality treatments, but its online presence failed to convey the brand's professionalism and trustworthiness. Potential clients often hesitated during browsing due to lack of transparency, resulting in low conversion rates.

### Core Pain Points

- The existing website's visual style was outdated, misaligned with the brand's premium positioning
- Treatment information was overly clinical — visitors without medical backgrounds found it hard to understand
- Lack of clear calls-to-action (CTAs) left visitors unsure about next steps

---

## Design Strategy

Through user research, I discovered three things that matter most to the medical aesthetics audience: **trust**, **information transparency**, and **booking convenience**. My strategy focused on:

### 1. Building Brand Trust

Used warm beige tones and soft rounded corners to create a "wellness" feeling rather than a "clinical" one, lowering visitors' psychological barriers.

### 2. Information Architecture Redesign

Broke down dense treatment information into a modular "one page per treatment" design, paired with visual effect illustrations so non-professional visitors could understand the value within 3 seconds.

### 3. Conversion Path Optimization

Added floating "Book Consultation" buttons on every treatment page, combined with social proof (client testimonials) to strengthen decision confidence.

---

## Key Design Decision

For color selection, I deliberately avoided the cold blue tones commonly used in the medical industry. Instead, I chose **warm beige and earth tones** as the primary visual palette.

This decision came from competitive analysis: 80% of medical aesthetics websites use blue-white color schemes, resulting in low brand differentiation. HEAL's positioning is "aesthetic wellness" rather than "clinical medicine" — the colors should convey **wellness**, not **clinical**.

---

## Impact

- Reduced bounce rate with increased average session duration
- Significant growth in online consultation bookings
- Client feedback: "The new website finally makes our brand look worthy of our pricing"
